Privacy Policy

Anubid Agency ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

a) When you send a quick message (contact form)

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Message content
  • Timestamp of submission

This information is stored in our secure database and emailed to our support inbox so we can reply to you.

b) When you create or sign in to an account

We use Google and Apple sign-in for authentication. When you sign in, we receive from those providers:

  • Your name (as set on your Google/Apple account)
  • Email address
  • Profile picture URL (if provided)
  • A unique account identifier issued by Firebase

We do not receive or store your Google or Apple password. Authentication is handled entirely by the provider.

c) When you submit a project request

Signed-in users can submit a project brief. We collect:

  • Business type (e.g. "Restaurant", "Retail")
  • Project description written by you
  • One PDF file (up to 2 MB) — typically your brief or requirements document
  • Up to five image files — references, logos, or inspiration
  • Approximate location — latitude and longitude, either detected from your browser (with your permission) or entered manually
  • Submission timestamp and the account it was submitted from

Uploaded files are stored securely on Cloudinary (a file-hosting service) and are automatically scanned for malware by Cloudmersive before being accepted. Submissions that fail the scan are rejected and are not stored.

d) Automatically collected information

  • Cookie preferences — your accept/reject choice stored locally in your browser
  • Basic session data — held in your browser's session storage while you are signed in

We do not run third-party advertising trackers or analytics scripts on this website at this time.

e) Email we send you

If you create an account, we will send you:

  • A one-time welcome email confirming your account was created
  • Occasional blog digest emails (up to three times per week) listing new articles published on our blog
  • Direct replies to messages or project requests you send us

You can ask to be removed from the blog digest at any time by replying to any email or contacting support@anubid.site.
